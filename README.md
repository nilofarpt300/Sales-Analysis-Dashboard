# Coffee-Sales-Analysis Dashboard (Interactive Dashboard creation using Excel)
## Executive Summary
Developed an interactive Coffee Sales Dashboard to evaluate sales performance, monitor key business metrics and identify revenue growth opportunities. Analysed customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and country level sales trends using Microsoft Excel. Translated sales data into actionable insights to support strategic planning, improves sales monitoring, and inform customer retention and market expansion decisions.
# Business Objectives
- Analyse sales performance over time.
- Compare sales across countries.
- Identify the top five customers.
- Track key performance indicators
- Identify potential opportunities for business growth.
# Tools and Skills
* Microsoft Excel - Data cleaning and data preperation.
* Excel formulas: XLOOKUP, INDEX, MATCH.
* Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts.
* Interactive Slicers and Timelines.
* Data Visualisation and Dashboard Development.
* Business Intelligence and Sales Analysis.
  # Key Performance Indicators
  * Total Sales Revenue
  * Monthly Sales Performance
  * Sales by Country
  * Top Five Customers
  * Product Sales Performance
  # Key Business Value
  The dashboard enables users to explore sales trends, compare market performance, understand customer purchasing patterns and identify potential growth opportunities.
